Play Chords With Your Hand

Hold your hand up to your webcam. Each finger you raise adds a note — thumb is the root, index the 3rd, middle the 5th, ring the 7th, pinky the octave — so raising several together plays a real chord. Move your hand left/right to walk through a chord progression. Runs entirely in your browser, no server, video never leaves your device.

Needs camera access and a click to enable audio (browser requirement).

How it works

  • MediaPipe's Hand Landmarker (WebAssembly) tracks 21 points per hand in real time from your webcam.
  • A finger counts as "raised" when its tip is far enough from the wrist relative to its middle joint.
  • Notes are synthesized live with Tone.js/Web Audio — nothing is pre-recorded.
